📧 User Instructions: How to Whitelist Our Emails

Share these instructions with users who report emails going to spam:

Gmail Users:

  1. Open Gmail and go to your Spam folder
  2. Find the email from EduBridgeSA (info@edubridgesa.co.za)
  3. Select the email and click "Not Spam"
  4. Add info@edubridgesa.co.za to your contacts:
    • Click the sender's name
    • Click "Add to Contacts"

Outlook/Hotmail Users:

  1. Go to Settings (gear icon) → View all Outlook settings
  2. Go to Mail → Junk email
  3. Under "Safe senders and domains", click "Add"
  4. Enter: info@edubridgesa.co.za
  5. Click "Save"

Yahoo Mail Users:

  1. Go to Settings → More Settings → Filters
  2. Click "Add new filters"
  3. Set "From" contains: info@edubridgesa.co.za
  4. Choose "Move to folder" → Inbox
  5. Save the filter

Apple Mail Users:

  1. Open the email from EduBridgeSA
  2. Click the sender's email address
  3. Select "Add to Contacts"
  4. Or go to Mail → Preferences → Rules to create a rule
